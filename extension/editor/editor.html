Keyboard & privacy

Choose a tool, focus the annotation overlay, and press Enter to add at the visible center. In Select, arrow keys move the selection; Shift moves 10px. In Pan, arrows scroll. Delete removes, Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl/Cmd+0 fits width.

Redact protects exported pixels. The local original remains until discarded or expired after 24 hours. Blur and Pixelate are cosmetic. Closing the tab keeps the saved draft until expiry.
