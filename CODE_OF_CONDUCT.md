# Code of Conduct

KoalaShot welcomes constructive contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful, specific, and technically honest.
- Discuss code and behavior, not people.
- Welcome questions and corrections.
- Protect screenshots, URLs, credentials, and other private data in issues and reviews.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive or bad-faith participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Shik3i/KoalaShot/security/advisories/new) or ask for a private maintainer channel in a minimal issue without sensitive details. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove content that violates this policy and will handle reports as confidentially as the available platform permits.
