Simple by design

From page to PNG in a few seconds.

KoalaShot scrolls through the current page, captures each viewport, and stitches the sections into one vertical image. The original capture stays available for your next step.

Privacy first

Your screenshot stays with you.

KoalaShot has no account system, upload pipeline, analytics, or remote configuration. Processing happens in the browser after an explicit action.

  • No accounts or uploads.
  • No tracking, telemetry, cookies, or history.
  • No remote code, fonts, scripts, or third-party embeds.
  • Temporary editor captures stay local and expire within 24 hours.

Questions

FAQ

Does KoalaShot upload screenshots?

No. Capture, stitching, clipboard operations, and temporary editor storage are local browser operations.

Can it capture every page?

Normal document scrolling is supported. Browser-protected pages and pages whose main content lives in an internal scroll area are not supported in Phase 1.

Are annotations available now?

Not yet. The editor page is a functional preview and export foundation; annotation tools are the direct next phase.

Where are store links?

Store publishing is planned for a later phase. This site does not show fake or inactive store badges.
