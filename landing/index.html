Privacy first

Your screenshot stays with you.

KoalaShot has no account system, upload pipeline, analytics, or remote configuration. Processing happens in the browser after an explicit action.

  • No accounts or uploads.
  • No tracking, telemetry, cookies, or history.
  • No remote code, fonts, scripts, or third-party embeds.
  • Temporary editor captures stay local, expire after 24 hours, and are deleted by an open editor or when KoalaShot is next opened.

Questions

FAQ

Does KoalaShot upload screenshots?

No. Capture, stitching, clipboard operations, and temporary editor storage are local browser operations.

Can it capture every page?

Normal document scrolling is supported by default. The popup can also capture the largest fully visible vertical scroll area inside a page. Browser-protected pages, partially off-screen roots, and horizontal stitching remain unsupported.

What can the editor do?

The local editor supports pen, highlighter, arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, multiline text, secure opaque redaction, cosmetic pixelation and blur, numbered markers, crop, selection, movement, undo/redo, zoom, pan, and edited PNG copy/save.
