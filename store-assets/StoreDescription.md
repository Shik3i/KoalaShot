# Store description — English

Primary submission copy. The extension interface is currently English. Paste the description below into Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons; permission and review fields are in [ChromeWebStore.md](ChromeWebStore.md) and [FirefoxAddons.md](FirefoxAddons.md).

## Name

KoalaShot – Full Page Screenshot

## Short description

Capture full-page screenshots, add annotations or redactions, and copy or save a PNG. No accounts, uploads or tracking.

## Detailed description

Save a long webpage as one PNG. Add a note, mark a change or cover private details before you share it.

KoalaShot captures the vertical length of the page you choose. Use it to keep a reference, report a layout problem or send someone a page with your comments on it.

Choose what happens after capture:

• Capture & copy puts the original PNG on your clipboard.
• Capture & save downloads the original PNG.
• Capture & edit opens the editor so you can make changes first.

Copy and Save do not open an editor. While the popup stays open, you can reuse the completed capture without capturing the page again.

In the editor, crop the image, draw arrows and shapes, add text or numbered markers, and highlight a detail. Move, resize or duplicate annotations, undo changes and choose a filename before exporting.

Use Redact to cover sensitive details in the exported PNG. Blur and Pixelate are visual effects, not a substitute for redaction. The temporary original is kept until you discard it or it expires.

Screenshots are processed on your device. KoalaShot has no accounts, screenshot uploads, tracking or analytics. Editor originals and drafts are stored in your browser profile and become unavailable after 24 hours. An open editor removes expired records; otherwise cleanup runs the next time you open KoalaShot. You can remove the original and draft immediately with More → Close and discard. Files you already saved and images on your clipboard remain yours to manage.

KoalaShot captures ordinary webpages and one fully visible scrollable area inside a page. Browser settings pages, extension stores and some built-in viewers cannot be captured. Very large or continuously changing pages may reach capture limits; content outside the visible horizontal width is not included. The interface is currently in English.
